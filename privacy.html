Privacy Policy

This policy describes Solitaire Velvet for iOS and Android. The game is published by Deniz Ece Karaal Isiloglu and can be played without a game account.

On your device

The game saves your current hand, statistics, progress, coins, rewards, themes and preferences on your device. It can read your store country to choose an initial language. Local purchase records include a hashed transaction reference to prevent duplicate delivery. The game does not upload these local records to a developer server.

Advertising

Unity Ads provides interstitial ads between hands and optional rewarded videos. Unity and its advertising partners may process IP addresses and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, app and device details, usage and ad interactions, and performance information. These data support ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Advertising identifiers are subject to platform settings and applicable privacy choices.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes payment. The game receives product and transaction information to deliver, verify and restore eligible purchases. We do not receive your payment card details. A No Ads purchase removes interstitials and leaves rewarded videos available by choice.

Retention and your choices

Game data stays on your device while you use the app. Reset Progress clears gameplay progress and keeps settings, purchase safeguards and the permanent No Ads entitlement. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes the local copy. Device backups and store purchase records are managed separately. For Unity data or ad preferences, use the privacy control on an ad when available, your device’s ad settings, or the Unity policy linked below.

Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we use your address and the details you send to answer and follow up. We retain that correspondence as needed for the request or applicable obligations. You can contact us about access, correction, deletion or other privacy choices. The game is for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children under 13.

This website

These pages use no advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages may process technical request data to serve and protect the site. Linked services apply their own privacy policies.

保存と選択

プレイ中のデータは端末に残ります。進行状況をリセットするとゲームの進行は消去されますが、設定、購入の保護記録、恒久的な広告なしの権利は保持されます。アプリの削除やアプリデータの消去で端末内のコピーが削除されます。端末のバックアップとストアの購入記録は別に管理されます。Unityのデータや広告設定については、表示される場合は広告内のプライバシー設定、端末の広告設定、または下記のUnityポリシーをご確認ください。
